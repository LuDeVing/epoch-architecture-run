K2: Frac-Connections: Splits the hidden state into fractions instead of replicating it, which gives HC-like multi-stream mixing with less memory.
K3: MUDDFormer (multiway dynamic dense connections): Computes dense cross-layer connection weights dynamically per position, with separate streams for Q, K, V and the residual.
K4: mHC-lite: Builds exactly doubly stochastic matrices as convex combinations of permutation matrices (Birkhoff–von Neumann), so no Sinkhorn iterations are needed.
K5: sHC (Spectral-Sphere-Constrained Hyper-Connections): Replaces the Birkhoff polytope with a spectral-norm sphere, which allows negative entries and avoids Sinkhorn.
